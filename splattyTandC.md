## 1. Description of Service

The Bot is a free, automated moderation utility designed to:
*   Track and queue posts in your subreddit that match a specific post flair.
*   Generate a daily centralized megathread based on moderator-configured titles and body templates.
*   Archive the bodies of the original queued posts as comments under the new megathread.
*   Apply administrative moderator actions (locking, removing, or stickying) to clean up the subreddit.
*   Pre-schedule up to 30 days of daily jobs to ensure service continuity.
*   Transmit status alerts and queue health logs to a monitoring Discord channel.
