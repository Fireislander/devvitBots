## 1. Purpose of the App

PermScheduler is a Reddit Developer Platform (Devvit) moderation tool that allows subreddit moderators to temporarily send selected types of newly submitted posts and/or comments to the subreddit's ModQueue, manage those restrictions on a last-write-wins basis, end them early, and optionally send status notifications to a moderator-configured Discord or Slack webhook.
